Use the [public reproduction command](../REPRODUCE.md) for offline review.

fresh source/security/QA checks and a new immutable tag. No workflow may publish
keys, restricted assets or private runtime journals.

under `workflow_examples/`; publication of its design does not authorize live
inference, secret creation or untrusted execution.
